Brian is an independent software engineer living in Brooklyn, NY. He is currently the owner of [Instapaper](https://instapaper.com/), and exploring new products, ideas, and spaces.

Previously, Brian was a Director of Engineering at Pinterest and DoorDash, where he led teams of ~80 engineers. Before that, Brian was an entrepreneur based in NYC. A chapter which started with a failed startup and was bookended by <a href="https://blog.instapaper.com/post/149374303661" target="_blank">selling Instapaper to Pinterest</a>, and <a href="http://blog.instapaper.com/post/175953870856" target="_blank">spinning Instapaper out of Pinterest</a>.
